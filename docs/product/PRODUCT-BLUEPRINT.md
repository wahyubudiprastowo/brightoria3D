# Product Blueprint

## Promise
Anak belajar melalui eksplorasi dan tantangan. Orang tua memahami perkembangan tanpa istilah teknis berlebihan. Tutor hadir saat dibutuhkan. Homeschooling adalah pilihan, bukan funnel yang memaksa.

## Three product lanes
### Mandiri
Assessment, learning path, six worlds, missions, mastery, evidence portfolio, parent co-play.
### Tutor 1-on-1
Tutor recommendation based on learning evidence, booking, pay-per-session/four-session pack, Zoom/Meet, tutor outcome updates learning path.
### Homeschool
Separate consultation and enrollment workflow for structured official pathway.

## North-star loop
Explore → Attempt → Feedback → Mastery → Parent insight → Next mission or human help.

## MVP exclusions
Formal school administration, payroll, NISN, full PPDB, PKBM back office, public social network, open chat.

## Success metrics
Mission completion, mastery growth, weekly parent insight views, successful retry, low crash rate, tutor conversion after justified recommendation, refund/dispute rate, child return without pressure mechanics.
